Liqor

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Liqor is an Android app that shows how far your Jupiter positions are from liquidation. This policy explains what the app reads, where that goes and what stays on your phone.

What Liqor reads

  • Your wallet’s public address, which your wallet app shares when you tap Connect wallet. Liqor never sees your seed phrase or private keys, and it never asks you to sign anything.
  • Public data about the open positions held by that address on Jupiter Perps and Jupiter Beta.

Where it goes

To read your positions, the app sends your public address from your phone to two public services:

  • The Solana network, through the public RPC at api.mainnet-beta.solana.com.
  • Jupiter’s position service for Jupiter Beta, at gum-api.jup.net.

These services have their own privacy policies. Liqor has no servers of its own, so we never receive your address or your positions.

What stays on your phone

Your public address and your bubble settings are saved on your phone so Liqor can keep watching after a restart. Tap Disconnect or uninstall the app to delete them.

What Liqor does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or email.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No signing, trading or holding funds.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps shows the bubble on top of other apps.
  • Notifications warn you when a position moves into Caution or Danger, and keep the watcher running.
  • Internet reads your positions.

Children

Liqor is meant for adults who trade with leverage and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
